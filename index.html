How it works

Ask, get a quote, pay, get it built

  1. Ask in DiscordJoin our Discord and post what you need, your budget and deadline in #commission-requests.
  2. Get a quoteWe reply with what's included, the price and a ready-by date.
  3. Pay to startPayPal Goods and Services. Payment terms below.
  4. Watch it workA video of it running in Studio before each remaining payment.
  5. We deliverFiles are sent once everything is paid. Clean, commented Luau.
Payment terms
  • Under $100: paid up front.
  • $100 to $500: half up front, half on delivery.
  • Over $500: three milestones.
  • Hourly work: $35/hour, prepaid in blocks of 5 hours or more.
  • Paid by PayPal Goods and Services. Files are sent after full payment.
After delivery
  • Our own bugs are fixed free for 14 days.
  • If your game changes later and something breaks, the fix is quoted as a new job.
How we build
  • Designed by Powla Games and built with AI-assisted tooling, then tested in Studio and finished by us.
